# Included studies

The strongest direct sources for the current paper are Patrick et al. (2025), Rottermanner et al. (2020), Wong et al. (2006), Thomsen et al. (2019), Ouverson and Gilbert (2021), Drey et al. (2022), De Lorenzis et al. (2024), TransceiVR (2020), and WebTransceiVR (2022).

The main cautionary sources are Van Orden and Broyles (2000), Li et al. (2021), and Drey et al. (2022). These help avoid overclaiming that 3D or asymmetric collaboration is automatically superior.

The strongest measurement and educational framing sources are Radianti et al. (2020), Paulsen et al. (2024), Makransky and Petersen (2021), Hart and Staveland (1988), and Endsley (1995).

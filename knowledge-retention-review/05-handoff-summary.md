## What the corpus presently supports

- At the individual level, retrieval practice and distributed practice have stronger evidence than passive rereading; optimal spacing depends on how long knowledge must be retained.
- At the team level, retention can be distributed: members need not each know everything if the team reliably knows who knows what and can coordinate access.
- At the organizational level, retained knowledge is embedded in people, routines, culture, structures, and artifacts; preservation therefore cannot be reduced to documentation alone.
- Forgetting is not uniformly harmful. Research distinguishes accidental loss from purposeful unlearning, so a retention policy needs to decide what should remain retrievable and what should be retired.

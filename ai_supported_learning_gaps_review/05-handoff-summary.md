## Search Criteria

- Search strings: See `01-search-strategy.md`.
- Inclusion criteria: Evidence must identify a gap relevant to instructor oversight, adaptive immersive support, scenario generation, learning analytics, collaboration, or durable learning outcomes.
- Exclusion criteria: Administrative-only AI, unsupported commentary, and studies that equate engagement or usability with learning were excluded.

## Priority Synthesis Directions

1. Instructor and trainee participation in AI design is still limited.
2. AI support is often individualized or predictive rather than integrated into instructor-led, temporally unfolding collaborative activity.
3. Adaptive learning content remains underdeveloped in immersive VR.
4. Educational AI often uses narrow traces and provides weakly explained inferences to teachers and learners.
5. Generative scenarios require grounding, constraints, and expert review in safety-critical domains.
6. Short-term assisted performance is too often treated as learning; retention, transfer, and unaided performance remain under-tested.

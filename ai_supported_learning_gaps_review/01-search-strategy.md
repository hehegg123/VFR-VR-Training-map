# Search Strategy

- Topic: Research gaps in AI-supported learning that an immersive ATC/ATM training system could investigate
- Review question: Which documented gaps in current AI-supported learning research can be meaningfully investigated through a domain-grounded, instructor-guided, immersive ATC/ATM training application?
- Search date: 2026-07-22
- Search sources: Springer Nature, ScienceDirect, Journal of Learning Analytics, PNAS, PubMed/PMC, ERIC, and university open-access repositories
- Time window: 2019-2026, with preference for recent systematic reviews and directly relevant empirical studies
- Language limits: English
- Document type limits: Peer-reviewed systematic reviews, meta-reviews, framework papers with worked cases, and empirical studies

## Concept Clusters

- AI-supported learning
  - Keywords: artificial intelligence in education, generative AI, intelligent tutoring, adaptive learning, learning analytics, knowledge tracing
- Human-centered instruction
  - Keywords: teacher-AI complementarity, instructor oversight, learner agency, explainability, classroom orchestration, co-design
- Immersive and scenario-based learning
  - Keywords: virtual reality education, immersive learning, adaptive VR, scenario generation, simulation-based training, multimodal traces
- Evaluation
  - Keywords: learning outcomes, retention, transfer, longitudinal, overreliance, guardrails, validity

## Candidate Search Strings

1. ("artificial intelligence in education" OR "AI-supported learning") AND (systematic review OR meta-review) AND (gap OR limitation OR future research)
2. (teacher OR instructor) AND (AI complementarity OR AI orchestration OR human-centered AI) AND education
3. (AI OR adaptive learning OR learning analytics) AND (virtual reality OR immersive learning) AND education
4. (generative AI OR large language model) AND scenario generation AND (simulation OR safety-critical education)
5. (generative AI OR intelligent tutor) AND (retention OR transfer OR overreliance OR learner agency)

## Inclusion Criteria

1. Directly identifies limitations or open questions in AI-supported teaching and learning.
2. Addresses at least one capability relevant to the planned application: instructor oversight, adaptive support, scenario generation, immersive interaction data, collaborative instruction, or learning evaluation.
3. Provides enough methodological or empirical detail to support a specific gap claim.
4. Published in a peer-reviewed research venue or authoritative open research repository.

## Exclusion Criteria

1. Focuses only on administrative AI, admissions, plagiarism detection, or institutional policy without a direct teaching or learning component.
2. Makes general claims about AI benefits without empirical evidence or a documented review method.
3. Treats VR engagement or usability as equivalent to learning.
4. Describes a technical model without educational evaluation or instructional implications.

## Notes

- Borderline rule: Include broad AIED reviews only when their findings can be connected to a concrete feature or study design in the ATC/ATM application.
- Duplicate handling: Retain the most complete peer-reviewed version and exclude preprints of the same study.
- Access handling: Prefer open-access publisher copies, repositories, ERIC, and PMC.
